# Student Portal

The Student Portal is the React-based workspace for enrolled students. Your school controls which areas are enabled, so some features described here may not appear on your account.

## Command palette

Press **Ctrl+K** (Windows/Linux) or **⌘K** (macOS) to open the command palette.

- Search subjects, your enrolled classes, programs, and your enrollments. Every word you type must match.
- **View all** opens the paginated **Search results** page, where you can switch groups and page through matches.
- A subject or program match opens your class list filtered to that subject code.

## Dashboard

After signing in, the dashboard shows your current enrollment status, upcoming classes, announcements, and shortcuts to the areas you use most. First-time users see a short onboarding checklist that can be dismissed once completed.

## Classes

- Open a class to see **posts and materials** from your teacher.
- Submit **assignment submissions** directly from the class page before the deadline.

## Schedule

The schedule page shows your weekly class timetable for the active semester. Printable timetable PDFs can be downloaded when your school enables them.

## Tuition and statements of account

- The **tuition** area shows your current assessment and payment status.
- **Statements of account (SOA)** issued by the cashier appear here with download links. Each SOA carries a signed verification link so third parties (such as sponsors or employers) can confirm it is genuine.

## Digital ID card

Your **digital ID card** shows your student photo, identifier, and a QR code. Staff and verification kiosks can scan the code to confirm your enrollment status through the public verification page — no account needed for verification.

## Announcements and notifications

- **Announcements** from the school appear on your dashboard and in the announcements page.
- The **Notification Center** collects messages sent to you — enrollment updates, grade releases, payment notices, class suspensions, school events, and reminders.

## Help and support

If something is wrong with a record or you cannot access a feature, open a **help ticket** from the portal instead of sharing your account. Staff responses appear in the same thread.

## Profile and security

From profile settings you can update your information (where permitted), enable **two-factor authentication**, register **passkeys**, and manage personal **API keys** when your school allows them.

:::tip[Keep your account private]
School staff will never ask for your password. Use two-factor authentication and report suspicious sign-in prompts through a help ticket.
:::
