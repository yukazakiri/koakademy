# Optional Modules

KoAkademy ships six source-tree modules that extend the platform for institutions that need them. Standalone Composer modules can add further domains without copying source into the core repository. Modules are enabled or disabled per installation by your system administrator; some areas additionally depend on feature toggles in General Settings. If your school does not use a module, its pages and menus are hidden entirely.

## Announcements

School-wide announcements targeted at staff, faculty, or students.

- Administrators create, schedule, and publish announcements from the administration panel.
- Active announcements appear as a **banner** in the administration panel and on the **student portal** dashboard and announcements page.

## Forms

The standalone Forms module supports configurable surveys, evaluations, student
information updates, and other school workflows.

- Forms can require an authenticated account, identify a guest by e-mail or
  student ID, or accept anonymous responses.
- Administrators can define text, choice, date, rating, upload, and other field
  types, response policies, close dates, and review workflows.
- Responses can remain linked to the submitting user and can be mapped to
  approved KoAkademy student fields before an administrator applies changes.

Forms must be installed in the application image with Composer before it can be
enabled. See [Module Registry](../maintainers/module-registry/) for the
installation and deployment sequence.

## HR Management

The standalone HR Management module supports personnel records for institutional employees, internal On-the-Job Training (OJT) students, and external trainees.

- Manage permanent and contractual staff alongside work-immersion and OJT students without requiring portal accounts for every individual.
- Track manual attendance logs and process employee-submitted attendance correction requests.
- Configure institutional leave types, track per-calendar-year balances, and manage full-day or half-day leave requests.
- Conduct performance evaluations using customizable rubric templates with frozen historical criteria snapshots.
- Supervisors are strictly scoped to their assigned direct reports and team members.

HR Management must be installed via Composer (`koakademy/hr-management`) and migrated before enablement.

Out of scope in v1: payroll and compensation, biometric device imports, complex leave accrual/carryover engines, recruitment and applicant tracking, and dynamic multi-step approval builders.

## Cashier

A dedicated workspace for cashier staff, separate from the full finance administration area.

- **Student lookup** with Current Balance, History, and Transactions tabs.
- **Payment posting** against outstanding assessments.
- Works together with statements of account and the enrollment payment-verification step.

## Inventory

Property and equipment management for the school.

- Catalog of **products**, **categories**, and **suppliers**.
- **Stock movements** and amendments with a full inventory ledger.
- **Borrowings** — track equipment lent to staff or students and its return status.

## Library System

Library operations for school librarians.

- **Books**, **authors**, and **categories** catalog management.
- **Borrow records** — check books in and out and track overdue items.
- **Research papers** repository for the institution's archive.

## Notification Center

Template-based messaging to keep the school community informed.

- Prebuilt **templates** for common situations: enrollment updates, grade releases, payment notices, class suspensions, holidays, school events, reminders, warnings, and official announcements.
- **Bulk sending** to selected audiences, processed in the background.
- Delivered to the recipient's portal Notification Center and by e-mail where configured.

## Student Medical Records

Clinic visit records managed by authorized medical staff.

- Visit records with type, status, and priority, plus **follow-up scheduling**.
- **Confidentiality flags** restrict sensitive records to authorized personnel.
- A dashboard widget summarizes clinic activity.

:::caution[Privacy]
Medical records contain sensitive personal data. Access is limited by role and confidentiality flags — do not grant the medical records permissions to general administrative accounts.
:::

## Enabling or disabling a module

Open **Administrators → Marketplace** to enable or disable an installed module.
The Marketplace also reports the signed registry catalog and compatibility
status, but it does not install Composer packages or update a running
container. A standalone module must be added to the application, included in a
new image, and migrated before it can be enabled.

Existing module status choices are preserved during upgrades. The six bundled
modules ship enabled for backward-compatible installations; a newly installed
standalone module may appear disabled until a super administrator enables it.
Some areas additionally depend on feature toggles in **General Settings** —
for example the inventory and library modules, the student portal, online
enrollment, clearances, signature capture, and QR codes.
