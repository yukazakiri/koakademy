# Availability, eligibility, and required documents

These stages decide whether a student may begin or continue the enrollment journey.

## Availability

Availability answers **when and where enrollment can start**.

### Enrollment channels

Select every supported entry path: public registration, administrator-assisted enrollment, continuing enrollment, or API. If the policy engine is not active, legacy channel settings remain authoritative.

### Date window

Choose an opening date, closing date, both, or neither. Leaving the opening date blank means enrollment is available immediately; leaving the closing date blank means it has no policy closing date.

:::note[What this does]
Students outside the configured channel or date window are blocked before assignment, billing, or approvals begin. Simulation shows the exact blocker without creating an enrollment.
:::

## Eligibility

Eligibility answers **whether this student is allowed to enroll**. Common checks include:

- Allowed student types, schools, programs, periods, and year levels
- Clearance or verified records
- Duplicate enrollment in the same academic period
- Prerequisites and grades
- Outstanding balance
- Unit limit
- Class capacity
- Schedule conflict

Use a check only when its required data is reliable. Rules fail closed: if a required fact cannot be obtained, the student is blocked with an actionable message instead of being silently allowed through.

### Sentence-style editing

Each card states the behavior in plain language. Turn it on, choose accepted values, and use **What this does** for impact and examples. Technical identifiers are hidden from the normal workflow.

### Empty allowed lists

For built-in “allowed” checks, an empty selection means all values are accepted. Prefer leaving a check unconfigured over selecting every existing program if future programs should also be allowed.

## Required documents

Each document has:

- A student-facing name
- Instructions describing format and submission expectations
- A required switch
- The workflow step that enforces it
- An automatically generated stable key

Each new policy enrollment receives its own requirement records. Before entering an enforcement step, every required item assigned to that step must be verified or explicitly waived by an authorized actor. A waiver always records the reason, actor, time, and workflow event.

:::tip[Write instructions as the student sees them]
“Upload a clear PDF of both sides; maximum 5 MB” is more useful than “Submit valid ID.”
:::

## Common scenarios

### Online enrollment only during May

Allow the public channel and set May 1 through May 31. Keep the administrator channel available only if staff should be able to assist outside that window through a separate matching policy.

### Block students with an outstanding balance

Enable the outstanding-balance rule and provide a corrective message such as “Settle or arrange your previous balance with the cashier.” Simulate a clear account and an account with a balance.

### Different documents by student type

Keep universal documents global. Add transferee records in a Transferee scope and senior-high records in the matching student-type scope.

## Troubleshooting blockers

If simulation says a fact is unavailable, confirm that the handler’s source service is configured and that the representative student contains the expected data. Do not weaken a rule simply to make the simulation green; fix the data source or remove the rule intentionally.
