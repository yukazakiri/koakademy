# Faculty Portal

The Faculty Portal is the React-based workspace for teaching staff. The pages available to you depend on the feature toggles your administrators have enabled; if an area described here is missing, ask an administrator to review the faculty feature toggles.

## Command palette

Press **Ctrl+K** (Windows/Linux) or **⌘K** (macOS) to open the command palette.

- Search the classes you teach and the students enrolled in them. Every word you type must match, so `Mercado Alicia`, `Alicia Mercado`, and `Mercado, Alicia` all find the same student.
- **View all** opens the paginated **Search results** page, where you can switch between classes and students and page through matches.
- Selecting a class opens its workspace; selecting a student opens a quick profile card.

## Dashboard and action center

- The **dashboard** summarizes your teaching load, upcoming classes, and items that need attention.
- The **action center** collects pending work — submissions to grade, attendance to complete, and requests awaiting your response — so you can clear tasks from one place.

## Classes

Each of your assigned classes has its own workspace:

- **Posts and materials** — publish announcements, lessons, and resources to the class stream.
- **Submissions** — review student assignment submissions, grade them, and return feedback.
- **People** — view the enrolled roster and manage students in your section, including section moves where permitted.
- **Grading** — encode and submit grades per grading period. Submitted grades follow the school's verification workflow before they become final.

Class rosters and reports can be exported as PDF from the class area.

## Attendance

- Open an **attendance session** for a class meeting and mark students present, late, absent, or excused.
- Review **attendance records** per class and export attendance reports for your own records or for submission.

## Schedule

The schedule area shows your teaching timetable in two views:

- **Agenda view** — a chronological list of upcoming classes.
- **Bird's-eye view** — the full week at a glance, useful for spotting gaps and conflicts.

## Grades

The grades area lists your classes per grading period. Encode scores, then **submit** them for verification. Once submitted, changes go through the authorized process rather than direct edits — contact your registrar if a submitted grade needs correction.

## Profile and security

From your profile settings you can enable **two-factor authentication** (authenticator app or e-mail code) and register **passkeys** for faster, phishing-resistant sign-in. API keys for personal integrations are also managed here when permitted.

:::note[What this does]
Enabling two-factor authentication protects student data in your classes. It applies to your account only and takes effect at your next sign-in.
:::
